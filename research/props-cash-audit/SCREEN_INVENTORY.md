## Not inspected (and why)
- **Game-log table tab, Trends page, Pro features:** no table/log tab exists on the player page (it is chart-first; the odds table and recent-games tables sit lower). "Trends" on the player page is the back-link to the screener list, not a separate page. Marked as checked, not found.
- **Saving a filter combo or starring a prop:** skipped on purpose (writes to the account).
- **Mobile bottom-sheet behaviours** (the line stepper and Alt Lines on mobile), **sort controls**, **hit-rate slider drag:** not exercised.
- **Real-device mobile:** only a 390 px frame, see above.
